Welcom to Goa

Goa is India's smallest state by area, famous for its long sandy beaches, rich Portuguese heritage, and vibrant coastal culture. Located on the southwestern coast of India, it is bound by Maharashtra to the north, Karnataka to the east and south, and the Arabian Sea to the west.

Place To visit

  1. Fort Aguada

    A 17th-century Portuguese fortress with an old lighthouse and panoramic sea views.

  2. Baga

    Popular coastal hubs known for water sports, beach shacks, and nightlife.

  3. Chapora Fort

    A hilltop fort overlooking the Vagator coastline, famous for sunset views.

  4. Fontainhas (quarter)

    Fontainhas (Portuguese: Bairro das Fontainhas) is an old Latin quarter in Panjim, capital city of the state of Goa, India.

  5. Palolem Beach

    is a stunning, 1.5-kilometer crescent-shaped bay with soft white sand and calm, shallow waters located in Canacona, South Goa

  6. Cabo de Rama Fort South Goa

    Praça do Cabo de Rama, also known as Cabo de Rama Fort, is a medieval hillfort located in Khola village, on the coast of Canacona, Goa in India.

Things to DO

  1. Best Beaches

    • North Goa: Visit Baga, Calangute, and Anjuna for energetic crowds, beach shacks, and water sports.
    • South Goa: Relax at Palolem, Agonda, and Cavelossim for quieter shores and calm scenery.
    • Visit Historic Churches: See old architecture at the Basilica of Bom Jesus and the Se Cathedral in Old Goa.
    • Go on a Waterfall Trip: Take a jeep safari through the jungle to see the massive Dudhsagar Falls.
    • Shop at Night Markets: Browse clothes, crafts, and food at the Anjuna Flea Market or Arpora Night Market.
